# Image Processing — ME01000681

**Source document:** GTU M.E., Semester 1, academic year 2024–25. 45 hours, 100% total unit weightage.

| Unit | Main topics | Hours | Weightage |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Digital image fundamentals; light/electromagnetic spectrum; processing-system components; image formation/digitization; grayscale and colour images; sampling/quantization; pixel neighbourhoods, adjacency/connectivity, regions/boundaries and distance measures | 7 | 15% |
| 2 | Spatial enhancement: gray-level transformations, histograms, arithmetic/logic operations, smoothing and sharpening filters; Fourier transform; frequency-domain smoothing and sharpening | 10 | 20% |
| 3 | Noise models; mean/order-statistics/adaptive filters; band-reject/pass/notch filters; spatial/frequency restoration; degradation estimation; inverse filtering | 6 | 15% |
| 4 | Colour fundamentals/models/transforms; colour smoothing, sharpening and segmentation | 4 | 12% |
| 5 | Image pyramids; multiresolution expansion; wavelet and Haar transforms | 3 | 7% |
| 6 | Compression models; lossless and lossy compression | 4 | 10% |
| 7 | Morphology: dilation, erosion, opening, closing, hit-or-miss, boundary extraction, region filling, connected components, thinning and thickening | 7 | 14% |
| 8 | Edge/region-based segmentation; split-and-merge; discontinuity detection; edge linking/boundaries; thresholding | 4 | 7% |

**Assessment shown in source:** 3 lecture hours + 2 practical hours; 4 credits; total 150 marks.

**Suggested texts listed in source:** Gonzalez and Woods, *Digital Image Processing*; Chanda and Majumder, *Digital Image Processing*; Anil K. Jain, *Fundamentals of Digital Image Processing*.
