**Source document:** GTU PG, Semester 1, academic year 2024–25. Course category MLC. The source lists 15 hours and 2 credits; assessment shown is practical PA/CA 20 and practical ESE 80 (100 total).

**Course outcomes in source:** explain research phases and ethics; explain literature review, gaps and objectives; identify a relevant original problem and methods; identify models for a research area; relate IPR protection to research and products.

**Suggested resources listed in source:** C. R. Kothari, *Research Methodology: Methods and Techniques*; Ranjit Kumar, *Research Methodology: A Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners*; Creswell, *Research Design*; and K. V. Nithyananda, *Intellectual Property Rights: Protection and Management*.

The source asks students to review papers from reputable international journals in three parts: summary, critical evaluation and creative synthesis.
