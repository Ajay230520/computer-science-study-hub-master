# Probability and Statistics Case Studies

For each case, record the decision, uncertainty, evidence, probability model, assumptions, cost of errors, and limitations.

Potential tracks:
- Rare-event detection and class imbalance in medical, infrastructure, and fraud classification.
- Defect-rate estimation and quality sampling.
- Reliability and component failure.
- Model confidence calibration and decision thresholds.

Do not describe a potential case as a documented real incident until a reliable source is verified. Research summaries belong in [Research and Case Studies](../../../../research-and-case-studies/README.md).
