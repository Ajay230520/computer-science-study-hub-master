# Probability and Statistics

**Status:** In progress  
**Prerequisites:** fractions, ratios, percentages, basic algebra  
**Goal:** reason under uncertainty and understand statistical foundations of ML.

| Stage | Topic | Core question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Probability foundations | What does probability measure? |
| 2 | Outcomes, sample spaces, events | What exactly are we counting? |
| 3 | Counting principles | How many possible cases exist? |
| 4 | Addition rule | What is the chance of A or B? |
| 5 | Multiplication rule | What is the chance of A and B? |
| 6 | Conditional probability | How does known information affect probability? |
| 7 | Independence | Does knowing B change the chance of A? |
| 8 | Bayes' theorem | How should evidence update belief? |
| 9 | Random variables | How do we represent uncertain numbers? |
| 10 | Discrete and continuous distributions | Which probability model fits? |
| 11 | Expectation and variance | What is typical and how much does it vary? |
| 12 | Joint probability, covariance, correlation | How do variables relate? |
| 13 | Sampling and estimation | What can samples tell us? |
| 14 | Confidence intervals and tests | How strong is the evidence? |
| 15 | Likelihood and maximum likelihood | Which parameters best explain observed data? |
| 16 | ML evaluation and uncertainty | How do probabilities guide decisions? |
