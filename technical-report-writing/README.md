# Technical Report Writing

**Role in Semester 1:** Supporting academic subject  
**Status:** Roadmap established; detailed notes to be added as studied.

## Learning outcomes
- Organize a technical report for a clear audience and purpose.
- Explain methods and results precisely without overstating evidence.
- Use figures, tables, equations, citations, and references consistently.
- Avoid plagiarism and distinguish paraphrase, quotation, data, and personal interpretation.
- Produce readable reports and presentations for technical and research work.

## Roadmap
1. Purpose, audience, scope, and report planning.
2. Technical style: clarity, concision, terminology, grammar, and logical flow.
3. Report structure: title, abstract, keywords, introduction, related work, methods, results, discussion, conclusion, references, appendices.
4. Tables, figures, captions, equations, units, and cross-references.
5. Citations and reference management; citation styles required by the course or venue.
6. Literature synthesis and paraphrasing; plagiarism, attribution, and research integrity.
7. Formatting and templates, including IEEE-style papers when required.
8. Writing results and limitations without unsupported claims.
9. Slides, oral presentation, and responding to feedback.
10. Editing checklist and peer review.

## Practical deliverables
- A one-page technical summary.
- A properly cited literature review.
- A mini research report based on a reproducible experiment.
- A short presentation explaining the question, method, results, and limitations.

## Quality checklist
- Is the research/problem question explicit?
- Can a reader reproduce the described method?
- Are every figure, table, claim, and borrowed idea attributed appropriately?
- Do conclusions follow from the presented evidence?
- Are limitations and uncertainty acknowledged?
- Are formatting and citation style consistent with the instructor's requirements?

**Important:** Follow the exact rubric and style guide provided by the course instructor. Do not assume every assignment requires the same format.
