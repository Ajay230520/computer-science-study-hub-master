# Research and Case Studies

This folder is for evidence-based learning from research and documented engineering experience.

## Research-paper review
- Citation: authors, title, venue, year, DOI/publisher URL.
- Research question and importance.
- Method, data, evaluation protocol, baseline.
- Main results with metric and conditions.
- Limitations, threats to validity, reproducibility.
- What the evidence supports—and does not establish.
- Follow-up questions and potential replications.

## Engineering case study
Describe context, system, decision, constraints, evidence, outcomes, lessons, and alternative explanations. Link primary or authoritative sources. State clearly if an example is hypothetical.

Prefer original papers, official standards, official dataset pages, public engineering postmortems, and official documentation.
